<Note>Available on all plans (Free tier included).</Note>

Programmatic access to [Market Search](https://oddpool.com/search). Full-text search across prediction markets with filtering and sorting.

## What you can build

- **Alert bot:** poll for new markets matching keywords using `discovered_after`
- **Custom screener:** filter by volume, liquidity, exchange, category
- **Market monitor:** track all markets under a specific event
